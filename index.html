Abapo-Synth

Audio examples for “The Abapo-Synth: A Brazilian percussion texture synthesizer”

Abapo-Synth is a Max for Live device that combines a RAVE model trained on Brazilian percussion recordings with two granular stages: one in the model’s latent space and one on its audio output.

Each row below is one input sound processed five ways. Playing a clip pauses the others, so you can switch between versions and compare. All clips are loudness-normalized to −16 LUFS. The originals are mono and play on both channels.
